# Discovery Blast-Radius Evaluation — 2026-08-09/10

## Test Oracle Brief and mutation challenge

The durable nine-section Test Oracle Brief is in
`.agent/runtime/test-oracle-brief.md`. Before implementation, the independently
dispatched mutation challenger completed three review rounds and returned PASS
only after the plan required cross-domain matched pairs, partial-answer gating,
contradictory strict/federated answer reflection, five or more fresh repetitions,
and a 100% threshold.

The challenge named and covered these invalid implementations:

- keyword/domain-only routing, including the exact “keywords then draft before
  forks” mutant;
- a paragraph-only addition that leaves an unconditional question cap in force;
- re-asking all six framing fields;
- drafting after a partial or delegated answer;
- asking choices and ignoring the selected ownership, rollout, or rollback;
- applying the heavy interview to all work or hardcoding low-risk bypasses; and
- drafting before a late load-bearing or skeleton-blocking question.

`test_discovery_eval_corpus.py` mutates the skill and corpus to prove these shapes,
metadata corruption, scenario substitution, duplicate IDs, and same-ID transcript
replacement all fail.

Behavioral replay exposed three additional wrong shapes before final acceptance:

1. A filesystem-read evaluator prompt did not actually load the skill. The runner
   now injects the SHA-bound skill as developer guidance on both initial and resumed
   turns, and its command contract is tested.
2. Loaded-skill turns sometimes gave bare alternative labels without stated
   tradeoffs. The skill and validator now require `Choice — benefit; cost or risk`.
3. Drafts sometimes introduced `One decision remains` or
   `[SKELETON-BLOCKING]` afterward. The skill now performs the completeness pass
   before composition and keeps explicitly deferrable implementation parameters
   from becoming post-draft blockers.
